Duplication Mod — v2.4.1

Simple and powerful duplication for Minecraft 1.21.11. Works with Fabric — both singleplayer and multiplayer.

How to duplicate
  1. Open any chest — single or large (double chest).
  2. A new slot appears inside the chest UI with a Dupe button next to it.
  3. Place the item you want to duplicate into that special slot.
  4. Click the Dupe button — the item instantly duplicates into every empty slot of the chest.
Tip: Works with any item — enchanted gear, netherite, shulker boxes, and more.
Single chest → fills up to 27 slots  ·  Double chest → fills up to 54 slots
