Dupe Mod

✔ 1.21.0 – 1.21.11

Item duplication mod — works on 1.21 through 1.21.11. Fabric client-side. Singleplayer & multiplayer.

⛏ HOW TO USE
  1. Open any chest (single or double).
  2. A special "Dupe" slot appears inside the GUI.
  3. Place your item in that slot.
  4. Click the Dupe button → item fills every empty slot.
  • Works with any item (gear, shulkers, etc.)
  • Auto-detects chest size (27 / 54 slots)
  • Survival, Creative, Servers
  • Paid adds bulk-dupe & priority
